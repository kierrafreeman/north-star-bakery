Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to give the neighborhood a place to enjoy fresh baked goods. Our goal is to make good food while creating a friendly place for the community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe in using quality ingredients and choosing ingredients from trusted suppliers when possible. We care about making products that our customers can enjoy.

Meet Our Staff

Our bakery team works together to prepare breads, pastries, and cakes for our customers. Everyone helps make North Star Bakery a warm and welcoming place.
